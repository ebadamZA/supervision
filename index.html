Choose a topic that interests you or propose a related idea. Prepare a short, focused concept note and explain why the topic suits you.

01 CONCEPT NOTE

Outline your research idea

A short, focused concept note (one page).

  1. Working title and topic ID (if applicable)
  2. Research problem and why it matters
  3. One research question and 1–2 objectives
  4. Method: primary data, published dataset or replication
  5. Practical feasibility, limitations and expected contribution
02 YOUR MOTIVATION

Explain why it fits you

A personal motivation of no more than 250 words.

  1. Why this research question interests you
  2. Experience, interests and skills you bring
  3. What you still need to learn
  4. Why the project is feasible for your postgraduate studies
  5. What makes the findings useful
